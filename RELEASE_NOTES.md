Adam's Buddy Android AM06C watch test

Install the APK on an Android phone, then tap the Buddy icon. The header has Talk, Game, Daily, Watch, and More. Watch can send a phone alert that your existing watch app may mirror, save either a Buddy or arcade PNG for Watch Face Gallery → Custom, and open your installed AM06C watch app. More has Buddy Blaster, a small Doom-style original arcade game, and Buddy TV, which plays video selected from your phone. Use More for the optional 9 PM daily check-in notification too.

This is a test APK. Private sign-in and watch mirroring need phone tests. Some sign-in providers refuse embedded login. Buddy Blaster runs on the phone and awards score only, not cash. Buddy TV plays phone video without uploading it. The arcade watch face is a still image, not a playable game; the AM06C has no verified video or game installation path. The app does not yet send live PC or task alerts, and it needs the internet for Buddy Site content. Uninstall versions before 0.6 once; 0.6 and later share a persistent signing key. No API keys or PC bridge credentials are packaged in the app.
