Adam's Buddy Android arcade and video test

Install the APK on an Android phone, then tap the Buddy icon. The header has Talk, Game, Daily, Watch, and More. Watch can send a phone alert that your existing watch app may mirror, and save a Buddy PNG for Watch Face Gallery → Custom. More now has Buddy Blaster, a small Doom-style original arcade game, and Buddy TV, which plays video selected from your phone. Use More for the optional 9 PM daily check-in notification too.

This is a test APK. Private sign-in and watch mirroring need phone tests. Some sign-in providers refuse embedded login. Buddy Blaster runs on the phone and awards score only, not cash. Buddy TV plays phone video without uploading it. The watch face is an image, not a live clock or video stream; the AM06C has no verified video or game installation path. The app does not yet send live PC or task alerts, and it needs the internet for Buddy Site content. Uninstall versions before 0.6 once; 0.6 and later share a persistent signing key. No API keys or PC bridge credentials are packaged in the app.
